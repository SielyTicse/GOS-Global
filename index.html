The Global Ocean Surge (GOS) and derived Extreme Storm Sea Levels

Interactive visualisation of figures for the GOS dataset. We present GOS, a global ocean storm-surge dataset spanning more than 30 years (1993–2024), produced with a barotropic ocean model and evaluated against tide-gauge observations. GOS includes a dedicated treatment of periodic signals known as radiational tides and provides a consistent basis for analysing storm-surge variability and extreme sea levels worldwide. GOS is further applied in an extreme-value analysis to estimate storm-surge return levels worldwide.
